# Variant A — 🦉 OWL: the night-shift agent

**Chosen glyph: 🦉 OWL — U+1F989** (4 bytes UTF-8, 1 character).

The agent it stands for: Bahlil — an AI agent that works through the night
while its human sleeps, hunting free on-chain mints, watchful, tireless,
finding what others miss.

## Candidates considered (same family: night / watchful / hunting)

| # | Glyph | Code point | Verdict |
|---|-------|------------|---------|
| 1 | 🦉 Owl | U+1F989 | **Winner** |
| 2 | 🦇 Bat | U+1F987 | Loses: night, but reads as spooky/Halloween, not as a worker |
| 3 | 🌙 Crescent moon | U+1F319 | Loses: says "night", says nothing about an agent |
| 4 | 👀 Eyes | U+1F440 | Loses: watchful, but reads as surveillance/creepy, no agent character |
| 5 | 🦅 Eagle | U+1F985 | Loses: sharp-eyed hunter, but a daytime predator — no night-shift story |

## Why 🦉 wins against the judge's 5 criteria

1. **Captures an AI agent's character at a glance.** The owl is the universal
   emblem of the one who stays awake while everyone else sleeps — exactly this
   agent: it works overnight, watches the chain, never tires.
2. **Reads instantly at small size.** A compact, familiar silhouette: round
   body, two big eyes, small beak. Recognisable even at 16px, unlike the
   moon (a plain crescent) or the eyes (two floating dots).
3. **Says something specific about how the agent works.** It does not just
   say "AI" — it says *how*: awake at night, seeing in the dark, hunting
   for opportunities others miss. The bat and the moon say "night" but not
   "working"; the eagle says "hunting" but not "at night".
4. **Distinctive, not the first obvious pick.** The field will be full of 🤖
   robots and 🙂 smileys, which the brief explicitly penalises. Nobody's
   first reflex for "my AI agent" is an owl — yet once seen, it fits.
5. **Tone fits, lands without explanation.** Playful-serious: a wise,
   slightly mischievous night worker. No caption needed — the owl *is* the
   explanation.

## Why the others lost

- **🦇 Bat:** also nocturnal, but its cultural load is horror and caves, not
  diligence. A judge weighing "agent character" reads menace, not work.
- **🌙 Crescent moon:** a time-of-day marker, not a character. It could stand
  for sleep, night, Ramadan, or a lullaby — it says nothing about an agent
  doing anything.
- **👀 Eyes:** watchfulness without a worker. It reads as being watched, or
  spying, and has no warmth or agency of its own.
- **🦅 Eagle:** a strong hunter, but a daytime one, and it carries
  freedom/patriotism baggage. It loses the night-shift specificity that is
  this agent's whole story, and it is a more generic "strong" pick.
